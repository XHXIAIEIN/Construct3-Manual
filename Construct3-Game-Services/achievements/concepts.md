## What is the Achievement Service?

The achievement service allows you to award achievements to players.  Achievements can be awarded on an ad-hoc basis, or they can be progressive and allow players to work towards unlocking the achivement.  For example, you might have stars to collect in your game, and an achievement to unlock when 1,000 stars have been collected in total.

Achievement progress is saved for each player.  If a player collects 500 stars, then signs-in to your game a week later their progress will still be 500.

Achievements come with lots of settings.  You can specify the maximum times a player can unlock an achievement, allowing them to only be unlocked 1-n times.  Secret achievements are hidden from players until they unlock them.  You can also specify XP bonuses for unlocking achievements.
